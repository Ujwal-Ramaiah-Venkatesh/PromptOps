## 🎛️ AUDIO MIXING SPECIFICATIONS:

### Volume Levels:
- Voiceover: -3dB (primary, clear)
- Background music (optional): -18dB (subtle, not distracting)
- UI click sounds: -12dB (noticeable but not dominant)
- System sounds: -15dB (background ambience)

### Audio Processing:
1. **Normalize:** -1dB peak
2. **Compression:** 3:1 ratio, -12dB threshold
3. **EQ:**
   - High-pass filter: 80Hz (remove rumble)
   - Boost: 120Hz (+2dB warmth)
   - Boost: 3.5kHz (+3dB presence/clarity)
   - Cut: 400Hz (-2dB reduce muddiness)
   - High-shelf: 8kHz (+1dB air/sparkle)
4. **De-esser:** Threshold at -20dB, 6kHz center
5. **Reverb:** Studio room, 0.8s decay, 8% wet
6. **Limiter:** -0.5dB ceiling (prevent clipping)

### Export Settings:
- Format: WAV or MP3
- Sample Rate: 48kHz (video standard)
- Bit Depth: 24-bit (WAV) or 320kbps (MP3)
- Channels: Mono or Stereo

## 💡 PRO TIPS:

**Voice Performance:**
1. **Smile while recording** key moments (adds warmth)
2. **Stand up** while recording (better breath support, energy)
3. **Use hand gestures** as you speak (natural enthusiasm)
4. **Record in the morning** (voice is fresher)
5. **Do pickups immediately** if you flub a line (fresh energy)

**Technical Tips:**
1. **Record room tone** (30s of silence for noise reduction)
2. **Use pop filter** (reduce plosives: P, B, T)
3. **Position mic correctly** (45° angle, 6-8 inches away)
4. **Monitor with headphones** (catch issues immediately)
5. **Save frequent backups** (don't lose work)

**Editing Tips:**
1. **Use fades** between segments (smooth transitions)
2. **Remove breaths** where appropriate (not all, sounds unnatural)
3. **Match EQ** across all segments (consistent tone)
4. **Compare to reference** (other tech demos you like)
5. **Get feedback** (fresh ears catch issues)
