## 🎬 VIDEO EDITOR AUDIO MIXING:

### In CapCut:
1. Import hero entry video + BGM
2. Right-click BGM → Volume
3. Add keyframes:
   - 0s: 10% volume
   - 3s: 100% volume (SPIKE)
   - 10s: 100% volume
   - 12s: 80% volume (fade slightly)
4. Right-click BGM → Effects → Bass Boost
5. Export

### In DaVinci Resolve (More Control):
1. Import to timeline
2. Go to Fairlight (audio panel)
3. Add EQ: Boost low frequencies (60-200Hz) for thunder effect
4. Add Compressor: Makes drums punchier
5. Volume automation for dramatic build
6. Export with high audio quality (320kbps)
