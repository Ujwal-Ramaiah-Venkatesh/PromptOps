# 🎬 PromptOps 30-Second Video - 4 Clip Strategy
## Complete Production Guide for Google Vids

**Total Duration:** 30 seconds  
**Method:** Create 4 separate clips in Google Vids, then combine in video editor  
**Each Clip:** 8 seconds maximum (Google Vids limit)

---

## 📋 PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

1. **Generate each clip separately** in Google Vids (copy each prompt below)
2. **Download all 4 clips** as individual video files
3. **Combine in video editor** (CapCut, iMovie, DaVinci Resolve, or any editor)
4. **Export final 30-second video** at 1920x1080, 60fps

- **DaVinci Resolve** (Desktop) - Professional, free version excellent
- **iMovie** (Mac) - Simple, built-in
- **OpenShot** (Windows/Mac/Linux) - Open source

#### **Editing Steps:**

1. **Import all 4 clips** into your video editor
2. **Arrange in order** on timeline:
   - Clip 1: 0-8s
   - Clip 2: 8-16s
   - Clip 3: 16-24s
   - Clip 4: 24-30s (or 24-32s if Clip 4 is 8s)

3. **Add smooth transitions** between clips:
   - Between Clip 1→2: **White flash** (0.1s) - matches the screen shattering effect
   - Between Clip 2→3: **Smooth dissolve** (0.2s)
   - Between Clip 3→4: **Fade** (0.2s)

4. **Audio adjustments:**
   - **Normalize audio** levels across all clips
   - Add **audio ducking** when voiceover speaks (music -6dB)
   - Ensure **smooth audio transitions** between clips
   - Add **cross-fade audio** at transitions (0.3s)

5. **Color correction** (if needed):
   - Ensure **consistent color grading** across all clips
   - Adjust **brightness/contrast** to match
   - Apply **LUT** (optional) for cinematic look

6. **Final touches:**
   - Add **subtle background music** throughout if clips don't have consistent music
   - Add **sound effects** at transitions
   - Check **pacing** - should flow smoothly
