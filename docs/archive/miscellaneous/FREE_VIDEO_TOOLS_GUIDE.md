### 4. **Record Yourself** (Most Authentic)
**Free Tools:**
- **Audacity** (https://www.audacityteam.org/) - Free audio recording/editing
- **OBS Studio** (https://obsproject.com/) - Free screen recording with audio

**How to Record Good Voiceover:**
```
Equipment (you probably have):
- Headphones with mic OR smartphone
- Quiet room
- Blanket/pillow for sound dampening

Recording Steps:
1. Download Audacity (free)
2. Use USB headset or smartphone
3. Record in closet (clothes dampen echo)
4. Speak clearly, 145 words per minute
5. Edit out mistakes in Audacity
6. Apply these effects:
   - Normalize to -3.0 dB
   - Noise Reduction (capture 1 sec of room tone first)
   - Compressor (threshold -12dB, ratio 3:1)
   - EQ: Boost at 3kHz for clarity
7. Export as WAV (high quality)
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ Most authentic and personable
- ✅ Easy to re-record sections
- ✅ No character limits
- ✅ Completely free

**Cons:**
- ❌ Requires decent mic
- ❌ Need quiet space
- ❌ Takes practice to sound professional

**Step 1: Sign Up**
```

```

**Step 3: Paste Your Detailed Prompt**
```

Scene 4 (45-60s): Animate a timeline showing Black Friday disaster:
- 6:00 PM: Traffic graph spiking upward

Subtitle: '100% Preventable'

Scene 6 (75-90s): Fade to black, then show these questions appearing one by one:
'What if your cloud operations were...'
'PROACTIVE?'
'PREDICTIVE?'
'AUTOMATED?'
End with PromptOps logo and text: 'AI-Powered Cloud Operations'

STYLE:
- Color scheme: Dark blue background, red/orange for problems, professional corporate feel

- Music: Building tension, corporate background music
- Pace: Fast-moving for problems, slight pause before final questions
- Text: Large, bold, easy to read on mobile devices

TARGET AUDIENCE: DevOps Engineers, Cloud Engineers, Engineering Managers, CTOs

TONE: Urgent but professional, documentary-style, data-driven, builds tension then offers hope"
```

## 🆘 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: InVideo watermark too prominent
**Solution:** Frame your video content in upper 80% of frame, leaving watermark area less critical

### Issue: AI voiceover sounds robotic
**Solution:** 
- Use ElevenLabs instead (more natural)
- OR record yourself with smartphone
- OR hire Fiverr voiceover artist ($25-50)

### Issue: Stock footage doesn't match script
**Solution:** In InVideo AI, manually replace footage from Pexels that you downloaded separately

### Issue: Video quality is 720p not 1080p
**Solution:**
- Accept it for first version
- OR use Clipchamp/DaVinci Resolve for manual 1080p editing
- OR upgrade InVideo ($20/month for 1080p)

### Issue: Can't find good "cloud operations" stock footage
**Solution:**
- Search broader terms: "office worker computer", "data center", "typing code"
- Use screen recordings of actual AWS/Azure/GCP dashboards (your own)
- Use Canva to create animated graphics instead of footage
