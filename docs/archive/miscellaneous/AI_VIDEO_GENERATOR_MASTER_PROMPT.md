## PROJECT BRIEF

Create a 90-second professional B2B technology explainer video for **PromptOps**, an AI-powered cloud operations platform that prevents infrastructure failures before they happen.

**Target Audience:** DevOps engineers, Cloud architects, CTOs, IT directors at mid-to-large enterprises  
**Video Purpose:** Establish urgent pain → tease solution → drive demo requests  
**Platform:** LinkedIn (primary), YouTube, company website

---

## VISUAL STYLE GUIDE

**Color Palette:**
- Background: Dark blue (#0A1929) and pure black (#000000)
- Problem indicators: Bright red (#FF3B30) and burning orange (#FF9500)
- Money/waste: Gold/yellow (#FFD700)
- Text: Pure white (#FFFFFF) with 95% opacity
- Accents: Electric blue (#0A84FF) for tech elements

**Typography:**
- Font: Inter, Montserrat, or similar modern sans-serif
- Headings: Bold, 64-72px
- Statistics: Bold, 48-56px
- Body text: Medium, 32-40px
- All text should have subtle drop shadow for readability

**Visual Tone:**
- Cinematic documentary style
- Professional but urgent
- Dark, moody, tech-focused aesthetic
- Fast-paced editing (2-3 second cuts)
- Smooth transitions (0.3s cross-dissolve)

**Camera/Animation Style:**
- Slow dramatic zoom-ins on problems
- Quick cuts between multiple angles
- Screen recordings of actual cloud dashboards
- Animated data visualizations
- Kinetic typography for statistics

## SCENE-BY-SCENE BREAKDOWN

### SCENE 1: Cloud Complexity Crisis (0-12 seconds)

**Visual Description:**
Open with a wide shot of a stressed DevOps engineer sitting at a desk late at night. The room is dimly lit with blue monitor glow. Three large monitors display different cloud provider dashboards:
- Left monitor: AWS console showing EC2 instances
- Center monitor: Azure portal with cost management graphs
- Right monitor: Google Cloud Platform with red alert notifications

Red alert notifications stack up rapidly across all screens. Cost graphs show exponential growth curves shooting upward. The engineer's face shows stress and exhaustion, rubbing their temples or holding their head.

Camera slowly pushes in toward the screens as alerts multiply.

**On-Screen Text (appearing sequentially):**
- "Modern organizations manage..."
- "1,000+ cloud resources"
- "Across multiple clouds"

**Voiceover:**
"Modern DevOps teams are drowning in cloud complexity. Thousands of cloud resources. Multiple providers. Endless dashboards. And alerts coming from every direction."

**Music:** Tense, pulsing electronic beat begins. Dark, ominous undertone.

---

### SCENE 2: Reactive Firefighting (12-27 seconds)

**Visual Description:**
Split-screen composition:

LEFT SIDE (50% screen):
- DevOps engineer on emergency video call at 2:00 AM (show digital clock)
- Laptop screen showing terminal commands scrolling
- Coffee cup and energy drink cans on desk
- Calendar in background showing "ON-CALL" shifts highlighted
- Engineer looks exhausted, hair messy, in pajamas

RIGHT SIDE (50% screen):
- Animated downtime cost meter counting up rapidly: $5,600... $11,200... $22,400...
- Stopwatch/timer showing hours ticking: 1hr... 2hr... 4hr... 6hr...
- Incident timeline visualization showing:
  - "Issue occurs" → 6-8 hrs → "Detected" → 4-6 hrs → "Resolved"

Red alert banners pulse across both sides.

**On-Screen Text (appearing as callouts):**
- "80% of time spent FIREFIGHTING"
- "6-8 hours to DETECT issues"
- "4-6 hours to RESOLVE"
- "$5,600 PER MINUTE during downtime"

**Voiceover:**
"Instead of innovating, teams spend most of their time firefighting. It can take six to eight hours just to detect an issue, and another four to six hours to resolve it. Meanwhile, downtime can cost thousands of dollars every single minute."

**Music:** Intensity builds, adding urgent percussion.

### SCENE 3: Hidden Waste & Security Risk (27-45 seconds)

**Visual Description:**
Animated visualization showing cloud infrastructure waste:

- Dollar bills with fire effects burning and dissolving into smoke
- 3D animated cloud icons with resources inside: idle VMs spinning slowly with "UNUSED" tags
- Database icons oversized and glowing orange with "OVERSIZED 80%" labels
- Storage buckets with open padlock icons and red "EXPOSED" warning triangles
- Untagged resources floating disconnected with question marks

Cost breakdown pie chart animates in:
- Total spend: $45,000/month circle
- 35-40% section highlights in red and breaks away, turning to ash: "$17,000 WASTED"

Security dashboard shows:
- Compliance checklist with red X marks appearing: SOC2 ✗, HIPAA ✗, PCI-DSS ✗
- S3 bucket icon with open lock and "PUBLIC ACCESS" warning
- Calendar showing "280 DAYS UNDETECTED" for breach detection
- Misconfiguration counter: 70% of incidents

**On-Screen Text:**
- "35-40% of cloud spend WASTED"
- "70% from simple MISCONFIGURATIONS"
- "280 days to detect security breaches"

**Voiceover:**
"And the waste is massive. Thirty-five to forty percent of cloud spend is often lost to idle, oversized, or forgotten resources. At the same time, simple misconfigurations create serious security exposure, and breaches can go undetected for months."

**Music:** Adding dissonant notes, creating unease.

**Visual Description:**
Animated scenario sequence showing e-commerce disaster:

TIMESTAMP: 6:00 PM

- Server icons with red X marks
- Engineers frantically typing (small window showing incident response)

TIMESTAMP: 8:30 PM
- Website finally loads but "SALE ENDED" message appears
- Revenue counter shows final damage in large bold text

**On-Screen Text:**
- "6:00 PM: Traffic spikes 10X"
- "6:45 PM: Complete outage"
- "8:30 PM: Finally restored"
- "2.5 HOURS DOWNTIME"
- "= $840,000 LOST"
- "100% PREVENTABLE"

**Voiceover:**
"Picture Black Friday. At 6 PM, traffic spikes ten times higher than normal. By 6:45, the site is down. At 8:30, it finally comes back online. Two and a half hours of downtime. Eight hundred forty thousand dollars lost. The worst part? It was completely predictable and preventable."

**Music:** Dramatic crescendo, then sudden silence during "$840,000 LOST"

## COMPLETE VOICEOVER SCRIPT (90 seconds)

```
Modern DevOps teams are drowning in cloud complexity.

Thousands of cloud resources. Multiple providers. Endless dashboards. And alerts coming from every direction.

Instead of innovating, teams spend most of their time firefighting.

It can take six to eight hours just to detect an issue, and another four to six hours to resolve it.

Thirty-five to forty percent of cloud spend is often lost to idle, oversized, or forgotten resources.

At the same time, simple misconfigurations create serious security exposure, and breaches can go undetected for months.

Picture Black Friday.

At 6 PM, traffic spikes ten times higher than normal.

By 6:45, the site is down.

At 8:30, it finally comes back online.

Two and a half hours of downtime.

Eight hundred forty thousand dollars lost.

The worst part?

It was completely predictable and preventable.

Across AWS, Azure, and GCP, teams are stuck with disconnected tools, fragmented visibility, and no predictive intelligence.

Just reactive chaos.

But what if cloud operations were proactive?

Predictive?

Automated?

What if your team could prevent problems before they cost you millions?

PromptOps.

AI-powered cloud operations built for the modern cloud.
```

**Voiceover Direction:**
- Voice: Professional, authoritative, male or female
- Accent: Neutral American or British English
- Tone: Urgent and serious for problems (0-78s), confident and hopeful for solution (78-90s)
- Pacing: Moderate speed, slight pause after "$840,000 lost" for dramatic effect
- Emphasis on: "DROWNING", "FIREFIGHTING", "WASTED", "PREVENTABLE", "PROACTIVE", "PREDICTIVE", "AUTOMATED"
