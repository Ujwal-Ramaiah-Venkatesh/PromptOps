# 🎨 Canva Templates for PromptOps Video

## Quick Start: Create Your Video in Canva (Easiest Option)

**Time needed: 45-60 minutes**

---

## Step 1: Access Canva

1. Go to **https://canva.com**
2. Sign up for free (or use Canva Pro for better templates)
3. Click **"Create a design"** → **"Video"** → **"1920 x 1080 px"**

---

## Step 4: Add Animations

For each scene in Canva:
1. Click element → **"Animate"** button
2. Choose animation:
   - **Fade**: For text appearing
   - **Rise**: For title cards
   - **Pan**: For dashboard scenes
   - **Breathe**: For glowing elements

---

## Step 5: Add Transitions

Between scenes:
1. Click between slides
2. Add **"Dissolve"** or **"Fade"** transition
3. Duration: 0.5-1 second

---

## 🎨 Design Guidelines

### Color Palette:
```
Primary Background: #0a0e27 (Dark navy)
Secondary Background: #1a1f3a (Lighter navy)
Accent Blue: #00e5ff (Cyan)
Accent Green: #4caf50 (Success)
Warning Orange: #ff9800
Error Red: #f44336
Text: #ffffff (White)
Subtext: #b0b8c5 (Light gray)
```

### Fonts:
- **Headings**: Inter, Poppins, or Montserrat (Bold)
- **Body**: Inter or Roboto (Regular)
- **Code**: Roboto Mono or Courier New

### Icon Sources in Canva:
- Search "cloud computing"
- Search "security shield"
- Search "deployment"
- Search "analytics dashboard"
- Use "Line" style icons for consistency

---

## 🚀 Quick Tips

1. **Keep it dark**: Use dark backgrounds throughout for tech aesthetic
2. **Blue accents**: Cyan/blue represents "safety" theme for Motorola
3. **Consistent style**: Use same icon style (line icons recommended)
4. **Readability**: Large text (minimum 24pt for body, 48pt+ for titles)
5. **Breathing room**: Don't overcrowd - leave white space
6. **Smooth animations**: Use "Fade" for professional look
7. **Test on mobile**: Preview looks good on small screens too
