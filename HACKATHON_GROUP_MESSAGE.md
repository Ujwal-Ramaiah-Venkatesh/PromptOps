## 📝 CUSTOMIZATION TIPS

**Before posting, customize these:**

1. **Video Link:** Replace `[VIDEO LINK]` with your actual video link or embed
2. **@all mention:** Check if your group allows @all or use @here / @everyone
3. **Emojis:** Adjust based on group culture (professional vs casual)
4. **Your name:** Consider signing with your name at the end
5. **Devpost link:** Add your Devpost submission link if you want direct votes

## ⏰ BEST TIME TO POST

**Maximize visibility:**
- **Weekday:** 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM (lunch break)
- **Evening:** 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM (after work)
- **Avoid:** Early morning (< 9 AM) or late night (> 10 PM)

## 🔥 FOLLOW-UP STRATEGY

**After posting:**

**Immediately (0-1 hour):**
- React to your own message with 🔥 or 🚀 emoji (starts engagement)
- Be ready to respond to first comments within 5 minutes

**If someone comments:**
```
Thank you so much! 🙏 Which part caught your attention? 
Would love to know what engineers like you find most valuable!
```

**If someone asks technical question:**
```
Great question! [Answer]. 
You can see this in detail in the full demo at [timestamp].
Let me know if you have more questions! 💡
```

**If someone suggests feature:**
```
LOVE this idea! 🚀 We hadn't thought of that but it's exactly the 
kind of real-world use case we want to support. 
Added to our V2 roadmap! Keep them coming! 📝
```

**After 2-3 hours, bump the message:**
```
Quick bump 🔼 - the 30-second trailer is pretty epic if you 
haven't watched yet! 🎬 Would love your feedback! 🙏
```

**Next day follow-up (if good response):**
```
Update: Thank you all for the amazing response! 🙏
[X] people watched the demo
[X] feature requests received
Keep the feedback coming! 💪
```

## 💡 ENGAGEMENT BOOSTERS

**Make your post more visible:**

1. **Pin the message** (if you're admin/allowed)
2. **Add relevant emojis** to make it scannable
3. **Use line breaks** for readability (not wall of text)
4. **Highlight key numbers** (60 seconds, 48 hours, 3 clouds)

## ⚠️ AVOID THESE MISTAKES

**Don't:**
- ❌ Spam multiple channels at once
- ❌ Ask for votes directly (against most rules)
- ❌ Post too late at night (low visibility)
- ❌ Ignore comments (respond to EVERY comment!)
- ❌ Be defensive about criticism
- ❌ Compare negatively to other projects
- ❌ Post and disappear (stay active for 1-2 hours)
