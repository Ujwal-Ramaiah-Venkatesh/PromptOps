## 🎙️ MASTER VOICE PROFILE

### Core Voice Characteristics:
- **Voice Type**: Deep male bass voice
- **Pitch Range**: Low bass (80-165 Hz fundamental frequency)
- **Age Sound**: 35-50 years old
- **Accent**: Neutral American or British English (professional broadcaster quality)
- **Reference Style**: Morgan Freeman, James Earl Jones, David Attenborough

### Voice Quality:
- **Timbre**: Rich, warm, resonant bass tones
- **Clarity**: Crystal clear articulation, every word distinct
- **Authority**: Confident, commanding presence
- **Engagement**: Conversational yet professional
- **Gravitas**: Serious but approachable, not overly dramatic

## 🎯 VOICE DIRECTION SPECIFICS

### Pronunciation Guide:
- **DevOps**: "DEV-ops" (equal emphasis)
- **Thousands**: "THOU-sands" (slight emphasis on first syllable)
- **5,600**: "five thousand six hundred" (all words clear)
- **$2.4M**: "two point four million" (pause between "point" and "four")
- **35-40%**: "thirty-five to forty percent" (all numbers clear)
- **6-8 hours**: "six to eight hours" (deliberate pace)
- **$840K**: "eight hundred forty thousand" (no "and" between numbers)

### Emotional Modulation:
1. **Clips 1-2**: Establishing → Concerning (gradual darkening)
2. **Clips 3-4**: Shocking → Revealing (financial impact)
3. **Clip 5**: Urgent (crisis scenario)
4. **Clip 6**: Concluding → Hopeful (transition to solution)

### Vocal Techniques:
- **Bass Resonance**: Use chest voice, not throat
- **Breath Control**: No audible breathing or gasps
- **Articulation**: Clear consonants, especially "T", "K", "P"
- **Pacing**: Vary between moderate and deliberate for emphasis
- **Dynamics**: Use volume variation for emphasis (not shouting)

## 📊 TECHNICAL AUDIO SPECS

### Recording Quality:
- **Bit Depth**: 24-bit minimum
- **Sample Rate**: 48 kHz minimum
- **Format**: WAV or FLAC (lossless)
- **Microphone**: Professional condenser or dynamic
- **Environment**: Studio quality, no background noise
- **Processing**: Light compression, no over-processing

### Audio Characteristics:
- **Frequency Range**: Full bass range (80-500 Hz emphasized)
- **Volume**: Consistent -6dB to -3dB peak levels
- **Compression**: Gentle 2:1 ratio for consistency
- **EQ**: Boost 100-200 Hz for warmth, gentle high-pass at 80 Hz
- **De-essing**: Minimal, only if sibilance present

### Mixing Guidelines:
- **Voiceover**: Front and center, clear prominence
- **Music**: -20dB to -18dB below voiceover (background only)
- **Sound Effects**: -15dB to -12dB below voiceover
- **Ducking**: Music ducks 3-6dB when voiceover speaks

## 📞 TROUBLESHOOTING

**Issue**: Voice sounds too flat or monotone
- **Solution**: Increase dynamic range, add more emphasis variation

**Issue**: Voice too high-pitched
- **Solution**: Use pitch adjustment (-10% to -15%) or choose deeper voice model

**Issue**: Numbers unclear
- **Solution**: Slow pace on financial figures, add slight pause before/after

**Issue**: Voice doesn't match professional tone
- **Solution**: Use "newscast-formal" or "documentary" style settings

**Issue**: Voice lacks authority
- **Solution**: Boost bass frequencies (100-200 Hz), increase confidence in delivery
