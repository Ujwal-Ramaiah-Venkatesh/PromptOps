## 🎬 COMBINING CLIPS IN VIDEO EDITOR:

### In Adobe Premiere / DaVinci Resolve / Final Cut:

1. **Import all 12 audio clips**
2. **Place them on audio track** in sequence (Clip 1 → Clip 12)
3. **Add crossfades** between clips (5-10 frames, subtle)
4. **Normalize volume** across all clips (-3dB peak)
5. **Apply consistent EQ** to all clips (if needed)
6. **Sync to video** timeline markers
7. **Export final audio** as master track

### Quick Assembly in Audacity (FREE):

1. Open Audacity
2. **File → Import → Audio** (import Clip 1)
3. Place cursor at end
4. **File → Import → Audio** (import Clip 2) - it will append
5. Repeat for all 12 clips
6. **Effect → Amplify** (normalize all)
7. **Effect → Compressor** (3:1 ratio, -12dB threshold)
8. **Effect → Equalization** (use voice preset)
9. **File → Export → Export as WAV** (48kHz, 24-bit)
